01 / 06

On the same plane.

An identity bringing architecture and execution together.

Precision as a language.
Coherence as a principle.

Coplanar starts from the idea that design and construction should operate together. The identity translates this approach into alignment, typography and relationships between planes, balancing technical rigor with understated warmth.

The starting point is coplanarity: distinct elements sharing the same plane. A horizontal and a diagonal make this concept recognizable across different formats.
